# Recipe photo prompts for eatzunera.com

One photo per recipe, 18 in all. Generate each with the prompt below, save it under the exact file
name shown, and it appears on the site at the next build. No code changes are needed.

**Where to save:** `site/assets/img/recipes/`
**Format:** JPG, square 1:1, about 1600 × 1600 px.

When a photo is added, it replaces the drawings on that recipe's card and page, and the
"photo slot" placeholder disappears.

## How to use these in Gemini

1. Use one chat for all of them, so the light and surfaces stay consistent from photo to photo.
2. Paste the **style block** first, then one **subject line** at a time.
3. Reject any image with invented text, a logo, packaging, hands, or cool blue light.
4. Check the food matches the recipe: for example, black raisins (not golden) and in-shell
   pistachios where the recipe shows them.

## Style block (paste once at the start)

```
Photorealistic editorial food photography for a premium Indian dates and dry fruits brand.
Square 1:1. The finished dish is the hero, centred, filling about two thirds of the frame.
Surfaces: honed beige travertine, warm wood or a soft linen cloth. Background softly out of focus.
Light: warm natural daylight from the left, soft shadows, gentle highlights.
Palette: sand, cream, olive green, terracotta and warm browns. No cool or blue tones.
Props kept minimal: hand-thrown stoneware, brass or clay, and a few loose raw ingredients from the dish.
Shot on a 50mm lens at f/4, three-quarter angle unless the subject line says overhead.
No text, no logos, no packaging, no people, no hands, no watermark.
```
